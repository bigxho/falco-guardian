# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

FALCO Guardian contributors are expected to communicate professionally, focus criticism on technical work rather than people, and collaborate in good faith.

Acceptable participation includes constructive review, clear technical disagreement, respect for different levels of experience, and responsible handling of security information.

Unacceptable participation includes harassment, threats, discriminatory or degrading remarks, deliberate disclosure of private information, and publication of security-sensitive information in a way that creates unnecessary risk.

## Scope

This standard applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other official project spaces.

## Enforcement

Potential violations should be reported privately to the project maintainer through the GitHub profile associated with this repository. The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the project.
